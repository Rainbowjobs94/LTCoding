# Replit Capability and Documentation Policy

- Verify current Replit capabilities with official documentation.
- Do not hard-code historical plan limits, credit quantities, publishing duration,
  background-task availability, or product URLs.
- Distinguish Agent interaction credits, application runtime/storage/bandwidth,
  and external-provider charges.
- A skill cannot alter account entitlements, create credentials, or keep a stopped
  workspace alive.
- A preview URL is not evidence of durable production publication.
- A sleeping workspace is not a scheduler; checkpoints support later resumption.
- Do not assume Docker, privileged networking, fixed egress, TUN devices,
  virtualization, GPUs, or local-disk durability.
- Use managed workflows and the project's documented authoritative release gate.
- Use Replit Secrets and integrations rather than handling credentials in chat.
- Require approval for publication, DNS, environment, billing, and destructive
  infrastructure changes.
