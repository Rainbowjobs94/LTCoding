## Mandatory staged gate

Every slice is a one-way evidence handoff:

**observe → normalize → label → simulate → human review**

- **Observe:** identify the source, provider, instrument/event identity,
  retrieval and event time, license/attribution requirement, and immutable
  source fingerprint. Do not execute uploaded code or install archive
  dependencies. Read archive central-directory metadata and explicitly named
  manifests only through a bounded quarantine review.
- **Normalize:** convert approved observations into typed, finite, bounded
  records. Keep sports scores, schedules, standings, statistics, play-by-play,
  and separately approved odds distinct from crypto, stock, forex, token, gas,
  and prediction-market observations. Financial adapters remain separate.
- **Label:** attach evidence, freshness, confidence, provenance, privacy,
  license, dependency, secret-risk, and authority labels. Use
  `OBSERVED`, `SOURCE_CLAIM`, `PROPOSED`, `IMPLEMENTED`, `TESTED`,
  `DEPLOYED`, `BLOCKED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `CONFLICT`, and
  `COUNSEL_REQUIRED` without upgrading one state into another.
- **Simulate:** use immutable observations and a pinned rule version for
  deterministic, replayable paper outcomes. A simulation, paper point,
  provisional eligibility, ranking, forecast, or testnet receipt is not a
  balance, reward, sale, custody record, or real position.
- **Human review:** stop for designated human approval before policy changes,
  source excerpts, public claims, sale/entitlement decisions, provider
  activation, testnet rehearsal, or release. Record rejection, pause, reason,
  rollback target, and next review in the ledger.

If a gate lacks evidence, mark the capability `BLOCKED` or `UNAVAILABLE` and
capture the omission. Never silently substitute a source claim, sports result,
model output, or simulated result.

## Non-authority boundary

The orchestrator has no tool or permission to trade, bet, submit an order,
transfer, bridge, stake, mint, sign, custody assets, settle, issue rewards,
change wallet balances, publish source content, or rewrite code, prompts,
weights, economics, permissions, or policy autonomously. Agents are advisory
and read-only. Human approval is still required for every high-impact action.

- `CFT` is `UNAVAILABLE_UNRESOLVED_ASSET` until exact name, contract, chain,
  issuer, rights, market, custody model, pricing source, and legal review are
  recorded. It cannot be priced, staked, sold, transferred, or presented as
  value.
- `mcp-sports-hub` is unreviewed/unavailable until a pinned commit, MIT
  license/dependency review, provider/tool allowlist, isolated read-only
  sidecar, attribution, and outage controls are approved. It is never a
  financial price oracle.
- Polygon work is a **testnet rehearsal only** after exact artifact,
  least-privilege roles, policy hash, pause and rollback evidence are reviewed.
  Mainnet, live sale, real wagering, and spendable allocations remain blocked.

## Learning map and status

Record the bounded learning map as:

`Sources → Normalized observations → Feature vectors → Match score matrix →
Virtual roster → Event predicates → Paper stake → Outcome → Skill score →
Calibration → Memory candidate → Human review → New policy version`

Every record links to opaque source IDs and hashes rather than source bodies.
Use the machine-readable implementation ledger and its human-readable backlog
to distinguish `implemented`, `proposed`, `blocked`, and `unavailable`.
“New neural pathways” means a reviewed versioned strategy or policy candidate;
it never means self-modifying production behavior.
