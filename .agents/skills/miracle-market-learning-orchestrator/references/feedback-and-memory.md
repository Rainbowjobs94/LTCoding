# Feedback and memory reference

Score forecast probability against later immutable observations without
rewriting losing or rejected history. Track calibration, sample size, drift,
data quality, risk discipline, drawdown, and time horizon separately from a
paper win/loss. Recent wins must not erase long-term losses.

Learning produces an append-only candidate lesson with evidence IDs, sample
size, confidence, expiry, conflicts, and rollback target. A candidate is not
an active policy. Tests, privacy review, and human approval are required
before a versioned policy can change.

User-specific memory is consented, purpose-limited, compact, inspectable,
correctable, exportable, expiring, and deletable. Store no secrets, raw
keystrokes, health inference, hidden profile, private prompt, or sensitive
identity trait. Disable, delete, and expiry controls must stop reuse.

Feedback loops are read-only for agents. The agent may propose a lesson or
policy diff; a designated human accepts, rejects, pauses, or requests rollback.
